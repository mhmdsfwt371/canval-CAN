What changed
------------
Tapping one of the ten cards filled in the pickers and showed a result,
but nothing marked which card had produced it and there was no way back
except tapping the same card again -- a toggle nobody would guess at.

Now the chosen card stays marked for as long as its answer is on screen,
a Back button sits above the result, and picking a make by hand clears
the card. Nothing else changed.
